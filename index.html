HIGHLIGHT⛰ Mount Agung Sanctuary

Besakih Mother Temple

Bali's largest and holiest temple complex — revered as the Mother Temple of Bali. Gracefully perched at approximately 1,000 meters on the sacred slopes of Mount Agung, this magnificent complex comprises 86 temples with breathtaking panoramic views and profound spiritual significance.

Kehen Temple — 11th Century

The former royal temple of the Bangli Kingdom, celebrated for its impressive grand stone staircase flanked by guardian statues, leading gracefully to the temple's sacred entrance.
